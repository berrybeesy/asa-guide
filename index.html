Member Onboarding

  1. Apply & Join

    Submit the application on our ASA website and join our club on Campus Connect.

    You'll receive a welcome email after joining Campus Connect. Then submit the Team Interest Form.

Event Promotion

How to get students to show up.

Event Promotion

Five ways to fill the room

  1. 01Event Sign-Up

    Share a sign-up link (Partiful, Luma, Discord) so you can track how many people are coming. This also helps you figure out how much food to order.

  2. 02ASA campus channels

    Submit your event details (form) to ASA and we'll post it across our channels (Instagram, Discord, Campus Connect, possibly Handshake). Submit at least 2 weeks in advance.

  3. 03Classes & Faculty

    Email lecturers to share the event, or ask to announce it in the first 5 minutes of class.

  4. 04Physical touchpoints

    Print QR-code flyers, get them stamped at the Student Union, and ask the front desk where to post them on the bulletin boards by the 1st- and 2nd-floor exits and in Gardner Commons.

  5. 05Incentives

    Talk up the food, merch, and prizes to boost attendance and participation.

Event Promotion

Posters on campus

QR-code flyers

Print flyers with a QR code to your sign-up page and place them near design studios, computer labs, and campus cafés. Please include our logo somewhere on your poster.

Posting around campus

  1. Add the ASA logo anywhere on your poster.
  2. Take it to the Student Union front desk to get it stamped.
  3. Once it's stamped, post it on the bulletin boards in the Student Union and the Gardner Commons building.

After the Event

How to redeem a gift card on Adobe Community Program

  1. Open My Rewards → Gift Shop

  2. Pick "STUDENTS - GIFT CARD - USA" ($25 or $50)

  3. Click "View more info" and redeem

  4. Follow the steps, then check your email

    Past rewards show under History.

1 event ≈ $25

Each event recap earns 1,500 points, enough for a $25 gift card (1,250 points). Two events gets you to $50.
